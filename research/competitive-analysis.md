# Competitive Analysis — Assistive Robots & Mobility Products

## Existing Products

| Product | Price | What it does | What it lacks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hello Robot Stretch RE1** | ~$25,000 | Mobile manipulator arm; navigates home environments, can fetch and hand objects, voice-controllable via ROS2 | Very expensive, arm-focused not follow-mode, not designed around wheelchair users specifically |
| **Labrador Retriever Robot** | ~$2,750/yr (subscription) | Low tray robot that fetches and carries objects between rooms on preset routes | No autonomous following, no voice commands, fixed route-based, limited payload |
| **Luvozo SAM** | N/A (enterprise) | Social assistive robot for eldercare; navigates halls, delivers items in facilities | Facility-only, not personal-use, no individual customization, not wheelchair-aware |
| **Aeolus Robot** | N/A (enterprise) | Fetches objects, opens doors, charges itself; AI-driven home navigation | Extremely expensive, not consumer-available, no wheelchair-following mode |
| **Amazon Astro** | ~$1,600 | Home security + delivery robot; can follow owner, carry small items in dock | Limited payload, no voice-command object retrieval, not designed for mobility-impaired users |
